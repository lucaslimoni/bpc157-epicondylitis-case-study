# Methods and reporting standards

## Reporting framework and ethics

This repository is an **uncontrolled longitudinal self-report by one participant (N=1)**, not a registered trial, clinician-supervised research, or an efficacy study. It follows relevant elements of the [CARE checklist](https://www.care-statement.org/checklist) as a *reporting aid*; it is not asserted to be CARE-compliant. Details about clinical diagnoses, assessments and interventions are explicitly labeled as participant-reported unless source documents are available. Public data may identify the voluntary author/participant, but do not imply consent to publish unrelated personal health information.

### Rules for inclusion and provenance

- Use the original observation/assessment date, separate from the date first reported in conversation.
- Preserve source types: participant statement, participant-provided assessment image, retrospectively reconstructed diary, photograph of a label, or independent clinical record (if eventually available).
- Preserve missing data as missing, not zero. Do **not** infer daily injections or undocumented symptoms from a stated routine.
- Distinguish the wording of a symptom report from a validated score. Never transform an unusually precise subjective rating into laboratory or clinical certainty.
- Report only verified source identifiers, quotations and literature metadata. Product labels and syringe photographs do not verify injected chemical identity, sterility, content or delivered amount.
- Identify co-exposures, exercise, diet, changes in routines, recall bias, expectancy effects and natural history as possible confounders.
- No statistics intended to estimate population effects. Simple observed differences are descriptive and do not demonstrate causation.

### Safety and reuse

Unapproved or investigational compounds and unverified products present substantial uncertainty. The materials are **not instructions for sourcing, preparing or administering drugs**, and no regimen described here should be interpreted as a recommendation. No clinical efficacy or long-term safety can be established from this record.

## Outcome definitions and study-specific considerations

- Main descriptive outcome: **self-reported bilateral elbow pain**, noting the activity (rest, warm-up, curls, rows, bench press), measurement scale and time.
- Functional outcome: **self-reported tolerance of familiar training movements**, without objective strength testing.
- Exposure: participant-reported product labeled BPC-157 and U-100 syringe markings; volume scale arithmetic is not proof of delivered drug.
- Co-exposure: participant-reported retatrutide during follow-up, plus resistance training and diet.
- As of 2026-11-13, the participant described near-absence of symptoms, but prior and subsequent observations were not measured in standardized identical conditions. Structural tendon healing remains **unassessed**.
- A single better-tolerated fatty meal was recorded, but it cannot support any conclusion about gastrointestinal protection or drug interactions.

### Study question
What changes in perceived elbow pain and exercise tolerance did the participant report over time during exposure to an unverified investigational product? This descriptive question is **not** whether BPC-157 works.
